TRADITIONAL INDIAN RECIPE

Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

A flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with basmati rice, tender chicken, fragrant spices, fresh herbs, and crispy fried onions.

⏱️ 60 min Cooking Time
🍽️ 4 Servings
🔥 Medium Difficulty

🛒 Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander leaves
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Whole spices such as bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves, and cardamom

👩‍🍳 Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, salt, mint, coriander, and green chilies.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and fry the sliced onions until they become golden brown and crispy.
  4. In a large pot, boil water with salt and whole spices. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is about 70% done.
  5. Heat oil and ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot. Add the marinated chicken and cook until the chicken is partially cooked.
  6. Spread the partially cooked rice over the chicken. Add fried onions, mint, coriander, and a little ghee on top.
  7. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for about 20–25 minutes.
  8. Gently mix the layers and serve the hot biryani with raita or your favorite side dish.

💡 Chef's Tip

Use good-quality basmati rice and avoid overcooking it before layering. The rice should remain slightly firm so that every grain stays separate after dum cooking.
